# Revarity Brand Guidelines

Companion to `brand.json`. Tokens live there; this file explains intent so a
human or an agent makes good judgment calls when a rule doesn't cover the case.

## The feeling
Editorial, premium, confident, honest. The closest reference points are a
well-made financial brief and a high-end architecture monograph — not a
typical "passive income" funnel. We are selling a serious operating business to
people with real capital, so the creative cannot look like a get-rich-quick ad.

## Typography in one line
**Fraunces** says it (headlines), **Manrope** explains it (body), **JetBrains
Mono** labels it (eyebrows, data, tags). Never substitute. Never add a fourth.

## Color in one line
Ink and cream do the work; gold is the accent you earn, not the wallpaper. A
single gold italic word in a headline is worth more than gold everywhere.

## Voice
- Direct. Short sentences. No hype adjectives ("revolutionary," "insane,"
  "game-changing" are banned).
- Numbers are specific and labeled as estimates with ranges when projected.
  Never a single hero number presented as a promise.
- We name the trade honestly. "You fund it, you sign the lease, we run it" —
  the model is stated plainly, never hidden.
- No fake scarcity beyond what is true. "A few deals moving this month" is fine
  if true; invented countdowns are not.

## The line we do not cross on creative
We do **not** generate AI talking-head testimonials or AI UGC "influencer"
video. For a high-trust, high-ticket offer, AI-fabricated testimonials are a
brand risk, not a shortcut — and they're dishonest. Video social proof comes
from **real clients**. AI image generation is approved for: furnished-unit
visuals, before/after transformations, lifestyle context, and B-roll. See
`DECISIONS.md` D-03.

## Domains (locked — but see D-02)
All Revarity-operator surfaces live under `*.revarity.com`:
`revarity.com` (brand/leases/landlords), `partners.revarity.com` (client sales
funnel), `portal.revarity.com` (RevOS client portal), `deals.revarity.com`
(RevDeal marketplace). The lease-research SaaS brand is the open exception
under review — see the brand-separation memo.
