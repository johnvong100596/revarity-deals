The premise

This is tooling, not a campaign.

David runs the funnel. Vu owns content. Malcolm signs off on spend. The CTO builds the layer underneath all three: a creative engine that produces brand-locked, QA'd ad creatives at roughly ten times manual throughput, plus the lead magnets — the income calculator and the deal list — that the funnel actually needs.

The discipline is David's own filter: anything built on Claude either serves the SaaS or ties to what Revarity is already doing — otherwise, question why it's being built. Every piece here passes that test. The income calculator is the master-flow profitability calculator. The deal list is the RevDeal teaser. The engine feeds the live funnel. We explicitly rejected the "autonomous AI ad factory" everyone's demoing, because it failed the filter — no proven use yet, and the wrong tool for a high-trust offer.

✕ We do not

Generate AI fake-testimonial video

For a $12K–$35K offer sold to people with real capital, AI talking-head UGC is a brand risk and dishonest. Video social proof comes from real clients. AI handles static, before/after, and B-roll only.

✕ We do not

Let a bot spend money

The engine generates and QAs creatives into a review queue. It never publishes to Meta. Approving creative before spend is a money decision — it stays human, like sanity-checking a deal before signing.

The operating principle

Repeatable is a bot's job. Money and the call are ours.

— North Star —

"If a step is repeatable, a bot owns it. Humans only own (1) live phone calls, (2) on-the-ground property visits, and (3) sanity-checking top-tier deal numbers before signing."

Applied here: generation, QA, estimate computation, lead capture, nurture, and performance analysis are all bot. The one defensible human exception in this initiative is approving creative before ad spend — and every ad launch or pause. The loop proposes; the human disposes.

The sequence

Unblock the decisions, then build in parallel.

Phase 1 ships a working operator funnel with engine-made creatives. Phase 2 ships the learning loop and leadership visibility. No big-bang launch — every stream ships standalone value.

Who owns what

The human / bot split.

◆ Bot owns

Creative generation · image-prompt generation · the QA pass · estimate computation · lead capture · nurture sequences · performance pulls · variation spawning · dashboard assembly. Everything repeatable.

◆ Human owns

Approving creative before spend (a money decision) · the D-01 / D-02 calls · the discovery and close calls · confirming real market data before scale · every ad launch and pause. The loop proposes; the human disposes.
