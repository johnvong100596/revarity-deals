The conflict

Two offers, one funnel.

The same prospect would hear two different prices depending on which document we built from. That isn't a detail to paper over — it's the single thing that decides whether the $7,000/month in ad spend has a coherent offer behind it.

Blueprint (David) · ad copy + landing page

Flat $375/month

"$375/month flat fee. No revenue share. Everything in your name." Setup quoted as $12K–$35K, actuals, no markup. Clean, simple, one number. Built for a cold ad.

vs
Master flow · Stone 03

Four tiers

Entry / Core / Premium / Portfolio, with AI prequalification routing each prospect to a tier and a tier-specific proposal. Built to capture margin across segments.

If the ad promises a flat $375 and the closer opens with tiers, the prospect feels switched on. Conversion drops and every CPA / cost-per-call target in the blueprint becomes guesswork, because they were modeled against one offer, not two.

The options

Three ways to resolve it.

A

Flat-fee everywhere

Kill the tiers. One price, one offer, everywhere. Simplest to run and to advertise.

Trade: leaves margin on the table — no structured upsell, no capture of higher-willingness segments. The model the master flow was built around goes away.
B

Tiers everywhere

Run the four-tier model in the ads too. Preserves margin structure.

Trade: kills the clean cold-traffic hook. "Book a call to find out which of four tiers you qualify for" is a worse ad than "$375/month flat." Cold CPL suffers.
C

Two funnels, one truth Recommended

Flat-fee is the cold-paid hook — a simple, honest entry price that gets the call booked. On the call, fit is revealed and the right structure is proposed. Tiers stay the engine for SEO/inbound, where the prospect arrives warmer and educated.

Trade: two funnels to maintain — but they share the same backend, pipeline, and close motion. The flat-fee entry must be a real, deliverable offer, not a bait number.
Recommendation

Take C. Flat-fee earns the cold click; the call reveals fit; tiers run the inbound motion. One condition: the $375 entry has to be a genuine offer we'll honor, or it's a switch — and a switch costs more in trust than it earns in clicks.

Decision needed before any of the $7K/mo ad spend goes live

Until this is resolved, the Creative Engine generates pricing-agnostic copy and the income calculator omits its cost side. Both unblock the moment D-01 is decided.
